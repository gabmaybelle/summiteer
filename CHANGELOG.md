# Changelog

## Unreleased

- The mountains know how high you are: every mountain biome above the treeline counts as high
  ground, and summit height counts anywhere. Both heights are measured from sea level and can be
  changed in `config/summiteer.properties`, so tall-terrain mods work too.
- Mountain clothes to stay warm up high: a beanie, a parka, snow pants and wool socks, knitted
  from any wool and dyeable like leather armour (with the same protection), plus mittens for the
  hands slot. Each piece you wear slows the freezing, and the whole set with mittens keeps the
  cold off. Leather alone no longer saves you from the cold up high; in powder snow it still does.
  Needs Curios (NeoForge) or Trinkets Updated (Fabric) for the mittens' slot.
