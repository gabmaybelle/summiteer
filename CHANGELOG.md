# Changelog

## 0.2.0

Base camp: ropes to get back down, cairns to find your way, a tent to sleep in, and wind that
won't let you pearl past a climb.

- Trail cairns: mark your way up, find it again on the way down. A small stack of stones that
  gives off a little light and a wisp of snow; in a whiteout it chimes softly every few seconds,
  so you can follow the sound when you can't see. 3 cobblestone (any stone) and a torch make 2.
- The base camp tent: a bed you can carry. Right-click it to set your respawn, sleep in it at
  night, and sneak-right-click to pack it up and take it with you. In every wool colour: 3 wool
  and 3 sticks. Beds are left as they are.
- The wind takes your ender pearls. Above the treeline a thrown pearl drifts downwind: a few
  blocks off target up high, more on a summit and in a storm. Under a roof, or below the treeline,
  pearls fly straight. No more pearling past a climb.
- Rope and anchors: your way back down. Place an anchor at a cliff edge, facing the drop, and
  right-click it with rope: the rope unrolls straight down to the ground (up to 32 blocks). Climb
  it like a ladder, faster in climbing gloves (step up onto it even from snow or tall grass at the
  bottom); sneak-right-click the anchor to reel it all back in.
  Works on any cliff, ravine or build. Rope: 3 string make 4. Anchor: an iron ingot, a stick and a
  stone block.
- Peaks always count as high ground. Frozen, jagged and stony peaks (and other mods' peaks) get
  the mountain weather at any height, so a low range of peaks that tops out under the treeline is
  no longer calm, and the ice axe grips their rock. Meadows, groves and slopes still need to be
  above the treeline.

## 0.1.0

The first release: mountain weather, clothes to stay warm, and the gear to climb.

- Weather up high. Above the treeline, gusts of wind push you around (a whoosh and a rush of snow
  warn you two seconds ahead; crouch to brace, or get under a roof), and they push gliders even
  harder. In a snowstorm up high, or on a summit at night, the cold starts to freeze you; a fire
  or a lit campfire keeps it off. When it rains below, it snows on the summits, in a whiteout that
  closes the fog in to a few blocks. Wind, cold and whiteouts can each be turned off in
  `config/summiteer.properties`.
- The mountains know how high you are: every mountain biome above the treeline counts as high
  ground, and summit height counts anywhere. Both heights are measured from sea level and can be
  changed in `config/summiteer.properties`, so tall-terrain mods work too.
- Mountain clothes to stay warm up high: a beanie, a parka, snow pants and wool socks, knitted
  from any wool and dyeable like leather armour (with the same protection), plus climbing gloves
  for the hands slot. Each piece you wear slows the freezing, and the whole set with gloves keeps
  the cold off. Leather alone no longer saves you from the cold up high; in powder snow it still
  does. Needs Curios (NeoForge) or Trinkets Updated (Fabric) for the accessory slots.
- Climbing gear. The **ice axe**: hold it in either hand and walk into a wall of snow or ice to
  climb it (or bare rock, once you're above the treeline), sneak to hang on, and if you fall past
  one you slide down it unhurt. Climbing doesn't wear it out; it mines like an iron pickaxe and
  hits a little harder. **Climbing gloves** (leather, wool and string) keep your hands warm and
  climb faster. **Crampons** go in the feet slot: no
  slipping on ice, you walk on powder snow, and gusts push you half as hard. **Snow goggles** go
  in the head slot and let you see much further in a whiteout. Right-click the gloves, crampons or
  goggles to put them on, the way you put on armour, and they show on your character: goggles over
  your eyes, crampons under your boots, gloves on your hands, over any armour.
