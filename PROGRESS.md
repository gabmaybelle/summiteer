# Progress

## Where things stand

- **What it does:** mountain weather above the treeline (wind gusts, cold in snowstorms and on
  summits at night, whiteouts on summits), dyeable wool mountain clothes that keep the cold off, and
  climbing gear: an ice axe that climbs snow, ice and (above the treeline or on peaks) rock, climbing gloves,
  crampons and snow goggles in Curios or Trinkets Updated slots, worn visibly and put on by
  right-click. Base camp: rope and anchors (a rope down any drop, climbed like a ladder, reeled
  back in), trail cairns (light, and a chime in a whiteout), a tent in every colour (respawn, sleep,
  pack up), and summit wind that blows thrown ender pearls off course. An advancements tab of nine
  mountain milestones. Fabric and NeoForge on 1.21.1 (this branch; `main` is 26.3); the in-game tests run on both, on their own and with Tectonic and Terralith.
- **Broken:** nothing known. The rope, anchor, cairn and tent sprites and models are drafts.
- **Next:** the 1.21.1 port (Fabric and NeoForge; Gabby's pick for the first port now that the
  mod is on CurseForge and has been played), then the Stone shrine and the Cairn; avalanches and
  the other two shrines later. 1.20.1 only if players ask.
